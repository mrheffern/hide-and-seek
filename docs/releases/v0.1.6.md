# Out of Sight v0.1.6

This internal Android prerelease introduces the first experimental hider-speed rule and game protocol 3.

## What changed

- Hiders have a 2.5 m/s target speed. Three reliable seconds at or above 3.0 m/s starts a visible 10-second warning.
- Slowing to 2.3 m/s or below for two seconds starts 30 seconds of probation. Continued speeding, or another confirmed two-second sprint during probation, disqualifies the hider.
- Seekers and the hiding countdown are exempt. Match pauses freeze the timers, and weak or interrupted GPS suspends judgment instead of producing a penalty.
- Hidden Mode is unavailable during warning and probation.
- The app provides a live countdown, vibration, and a locked-phone local notification.
- Diagnostics preserve native speed, accuracy-adjusted displacement, evaluated speed, state transitions, and disqualification evidence. The analyzer reports those outcomes without exposing coordinates.
- The host can export diagnostics during hiding, active play, pause, lobby, or after the match.

## Install and compatibility

Install `out-of-sight-v0.1.6.apk` on every Android test phone. It uses version code 7 and updates the previous internally signed beta in place. Protocol 3 intentionally rejects v0.1.5 and older clients; update every phone before creating a new party.

This remains an internal, debug-certificate-signed prerelease rather than a Play Store production build. The speed thresholds are experimental and require the section N mixed-ability field tests before competitive use.
